Welcome to Doom Scroll Blocker

How it works

The extension monitors your activity on supported sites and calculates a doom score based on your scrolling, key presses, and time spent.

What is doom-scrolling?

Doom-scrolling is the tendency to continuously scroll through negative news or short-form content, which can lead to anxiety and wasted time.

Configure sensitivity

Adjust the detection sensitivity in settings to match your habits.

Benefits of taking breaks

Regular breaks help reduce eye strain, improve focus, and break the cycle of compulsive scrolling.
